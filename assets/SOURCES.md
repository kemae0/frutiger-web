# Artwork and references

The two wallpapers are original images generated with the built-in image-generation tool for this extension, then encoded as WebP. They contain no text or logos. No reference-site artwork is bundled.

- `aero-scene.webp`: a wide 2005–2009 nature-and-technology wallpaper: rich cyan-blue sky, soft clouds and sunlight, an aqua lake, vivid green hills, clear glass water orbs, bokeh and wispy aurora arcs. Calm center for the existing interface; detail at the margins. No UI, text, logos or watermark.
- `dorfic-scene.webp`: a wide daylight-white industrial abstract-tech wallpaper: orange concentric technical rings, white beveled turbine discs, cool silver geometry, parallel orange ribbons, low-poly shards, micro-grid lines, reflective surfaces and crisp shadows. Calm center for the existing interface. No nature, glass bubbles, UI, text, logos or watermark.
